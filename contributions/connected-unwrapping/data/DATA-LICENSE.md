# PHerc0800 example data and attribution

Data used here come from **Vesuvius Challenge — CT Scans of Herculaneum Papyri**, PHerc0800, scan `20250510225703`, volume `20250521135224` at 8.64 µm, acquired at ESRF Grenoble. The original catalogue lists the holding institution as Biblioteca Nazionale di Napoli ‘Vittorio Emanuele III’ (Officina dei Papiri Ercolanesi).

- [Official scan record and data links](https://scrollprize.org/data_browser/PHerc0800)
- [Dataset citation guidance](https://scrollprize.org/data)
- [Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/)

This example contains derived geometry, masks, comparison visuals and CT renders from that dataset. Changes include physically filtering the native cell complex, selecting a connected chart, fitting UV coordinates and rasterizing observed CT. Dataset and derived examples are shared under CC BY-NC 4.0. The software in `tools/` has its own MIT license.

## PHerc0175A example

The `pherc0175a/` example uses Vesuvius Challenge CT Scans of Herculaneum Papyri, PHerc0175A, scan `20250511125731`, volume `20250521115057` at 8.64 µm, acquired at ESRF Grenoble. The holding institution is Biblioteca Nazionale di Napoli ‘Vittorio Emanuele III’ (Officina dei Papiri Ercolanesi). The fixture contains derived geometry, UV and whole-cell conflict constraints from the local case. The figures show derived chart occupancy and observed CT.
