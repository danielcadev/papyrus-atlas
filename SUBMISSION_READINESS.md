## Remaining scientific limitations

The published virtual-unwrapping preference includes visible fibers and evidence against sheet jumping. **This contribution does not demonstrate either anatomical property.** Its figure shows measured mesh geometry, not ink, CT anatomy or a verified continuous papyrus sheet. A reviewer may request stronger anatomical or downstream evidence.

The principal validation is a same-scroll local development case. There is no new-scroll holdout, independent external reproduction, established community adoption or benchmark superiority over current upstream. Grafting consumes recorded conflict evidence rather than independently discovering a complete geometric conflict graph. The exact contact certificate checks added/source and added/added pairs, not unchanged source/source pairs or minimum clearance. Angle bounds are necessary conditions, not a full UV map.

The work can be presented for **technical consideration as local software improvements**. No claim that every scientific preference is satisfied, or that the measured change will be judged significant enough for an award, is warranted. It is not a First Letters or whole-scroll reading submission.
